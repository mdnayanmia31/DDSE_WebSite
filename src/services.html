Digital Topographic Survey

Our flagship service precision terrain mapping using advanced digital instruments including Total Stations (Sokkia, Kolida KTS442), GPS (Garmin), and Auto Level machines for engineering, urban planning, and construction projects.

We deliver comprehensive topographic maps with elevation data, contour lines, and boundary markings for land development, road design, bridge alignment, and infrastructure planning.

Use Cases

  • Land development and plot layout
  • Road, bridge, and canal route design
  • Construction site assessment
  • Government infrastructure projects (RHD, LGED, DPHE)

Equipment Used

  • Sokkia Power Set 2000 Total Station (Japan)
  • Kolida KTS442 R6 L6 (4 units)
  • GPS Garmin (USA)
  • AutoCAD 2D/3D Processing

Soil Test & Sub-Soil Investigation

Complete geotechnical investigation including field boring, Standard Penetration Test (SPT), and comprehensive laboratory analysis. Our in-house laboratory is equipped to perform 11+ soil test types.

Laboratory Test Capabilities

  • Classification of Soil & Bulk/Dry Density
  • Grain Size & Hydrometer Analysis
  • Atterberg Limit Test & Moisture Content
  • Specific Gravity & Permeability Test
  • Direct Shear, Unconfined & Consolidation Tests

Soil Equipment

  • 4 Tripods with Casing & Drilling Pipes
  • 5 SPT Hammers & 7 Split Spoons
  • 200 Shelby Tubes (Undisturbed Sampling)
  • 150 ft Casing Pipe (4" dia)

Road & Bridge Survey

Comprehensive geometric and structural surveys for road design, bridge alignment, and infrastructure development. We provide accurate cross-section data, longitudinal profiles, and alignment surveys used by RHD and LGED for national highway and rural road projects.

Our team delivers survey data compatible with road design software and AutoCAD for immediate engineering use.

🌊

Canal & Land Survey

Accurate boundary demarcation and canal route surveys for irrigation projects, land management, and legal documentation. Our surveys support water resource management, drainage system design, and land ownership verification.

Used by DPHE, Zila Parishad, and municipal bodies for water infrastructure and land development planning.
