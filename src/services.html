What We Do

Comprehensive Surveying & Engineering Solutions

DDSE delivers precise, reliable, and professional surveying, investigation, and engineering services for public and private sector projects.

Topographical Survey

Land survey using digital total stations for precision terrain mapping and infrastructure planning.

Soil Test / Sub-Soil Investigation

SPT test, bore hole drilling, and soil sampling for comprehensive geotechnical analysis.

Bathymetric Survey

Water body depth measurement and underwater mapping using advanced echo sounder technology.

Land Plotting & Development

Professional land subdivision, plotting, and boundary demarcation for residential and commercial development.

Structural Design

Reliable civil engineering structural calculations and architectural drawings for robust construction.

Quantity Survey / Estimation

Detailed project cost estimation, material calculation, and Preparation of Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

GPS Survey / DGPS Survey

High-precision satellite-based positioning for exact coordinates, georeferencing, and control points.

Hydrographic Survey

Detailed river and water body surveying for dredging, navigation, and coastal engineering projects.

Engineering Consultancy

Expert technical consulting, project management, and planning for complex infrastructure projects.

Volume Calculation

Accurate cut and fill volume calculation and earthwork estimation derived directly from precision survey data.
