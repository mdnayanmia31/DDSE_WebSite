Track Record

Featured Assignments

LGED (Local Government Engineering Department)

  • Sub-soil investigation for 45 Primary Schools (Patuakhali, Barguna, Pirojpur, Bhola & Barisal) - CHTDB.
  • Digital Topographic Survey for 25 Union Parishad Complexes.
  • Survey and sub-soil investigation of Kancha market in multiple Zilas.
  • Rubber Dam topographic survey (Amtoli, Barguna, Bauphal, Patuakhali).

DPHE (Department of Public Health Engineering)

  • Soil test for extensive WASH Block developments (117 blocks in Barguna, 160 in Bhola, 160 in Pirojpur).
  • Sub-soil investigation for 10-storied DPHE Bhaban, Cox's Bazar.

BWDB (Bangladesh Water Development Board)

  • Comprehensive Bathymetric and Topographic Survey in Khowai River (Habiganj, Sylhet).
  • Sub-soil investigation of extensive drainage networks (Sylhet & Habiganj - 130 km + 65 km).
  • Soil investigation for bank protection work along Tentulia River.

MES (Military Engineer Services)

  • Topographic Digital Survey and investigation for Lebukhali Cantonment (1060 Acres) - Multiple Phase projects.
  • Soil investigation for 10-storied to 14-storied residential and commercial complexes within Cantonments.

Telecom, Hospitals & RHD

  • Grameenphone: Topographic survey and soil testing for 600+ network towers.
  • RHD (Roads and Highways): Digital Survey for Pirojpur & Barisal Highways, including bridge alignments.
  • Ministry of Health: Site investigations for Upazila Health Complexes (Barguna, Barisal).
