Who We Are আমরা কারা

Trusted by Government,
Built on Precision

Founded in 2015, Dimension Digital Survey & Engineering (DDSE) has established itself as one of the most trusted names in digital land surveying and civil engineering services in Bangladesh.

Based in Barisal, with a presence in Dhaka, DDSE provides precision survey services to major government departments including RHD, LGED, DPHE, MES, and multiple municipal bodies.

Technology & Tools

Our Equipment

We utilize state-of-the-art surveying instruments to ensure millimeter-level precision across all topographical and geotechnical projects.

Total Stations

Fleet of 8 Kolida KTS442 R6 L6 and 2 Sokkia robotic total stations for high-accuracy angular and distance measurements.

RTK GPS & Drones

2 RTK GNSS systems for centimeter-level global positioning, paired with 2 DJI aerial mapping drones for rapid topographical acquisition.

Bathymetric & Soil Testing

Equipped with 2 Echosounders for hydrographic surveys, alongside complete geotechnical rigs including 300 Shelby tubes for undisturbed soil sampling.
