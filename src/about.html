Our Story

Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver Survey of Bangladesh Approved (Licence No. 6397) digital survey and civil engineering services with measurable precision — from topographic and soil investigation through to AutoCAD drafting — for government and private sector clients across Bangladesh.

Vision: To be the trusted survey partner for Bangladesh's infrastructure growth, already serving 15+ government departments including RHD, LGED, DPHE, BWDB, MES, and the Survey of Bangladesh, with 700+ completed projects since 2015.

আমাদের লক্ষ্য হলো বাংলাদেশে সর্বোচ্চ মানের ডিজিটাল জরিপ ও ভূ-প্রকৌশল সেবা প্রদান করা।

Company History

Founded in 2015 by Engr. Md. Ibrahim Akond, DDSE began as a commitment to bringing digital precision to the survey industry in Bangladesh. Starting from humble beginnings in Barisal, the company rapidly grew by delivering excellence in topographic survey, soil testing, and engineering services.

By 2018, DDSE had earned the prestigious Survey of Bangladesh Approval, establishing itself as a government-recognized survey practice. Today, DDSE serves major government departments and private clients across the nation.
