%--- PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY ---
\section{Professional Summary}

A dynamic and results-oriented Civil Engineer with over nine years of progressive experience
in digital land surveying, topographic mapping, and engineering project management.
As the founding Managing Director of \textbf{Dimension Digital Survey \& Engineering},
he has led the company from its establishment in 2018 to become a
\textit{Survey of Bangladesh Approved} professional practice, delivering precision
survey solutions for government and private sector clients across Bangladesh.
Combining technical expertise in AutoCAD, GIS, and field survey operations with strong
organizational leadership, he brings an end-to-end capability — from site assessment
and data acquisition through to report delivery and client consultation.
